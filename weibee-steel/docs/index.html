Findings

Computed from the marts, not typed in. Each has a next action.

  1. Where the shop fits. K-12 school, Church / worship, Retail / commercial win 37%-41% of decided bids and give back 3.0 pts or less of estimated margin. Sports / recreation, Hospitality, Multifamily podium win 12%-15% and give back 7.4-10.4 pts. Together the bottom three consumed 29% of estimating hours for 18% of dollars won. → Default the bottom three to Review in the bid/no-bid meeting; bid them only with a relationship GC or a negotiated scope.
  2. Pricing feedback. When we lose on price and get a number back (100 of 161 price losses), our bid is a median 9.7% above the low bid. On hard bids the hit rate is 20% against an average of 5.6 bidders; negotiated work wins 49%. When we win and learn the next number, we left a median 3.9% on the table. → Collect the low number on every loss (coverage is 62% today); a gap under 3 % is a markup conversation, a gap over 10 % is a fit conversation.
  3. Price shoppers. 7 general contractors (Ouachita Building Group, Pioneer Civic Builders, Osage Builders, Heartland Build Co…) have awarded us 18% of bids with a median price gap of 10.7%: 2,650 estimating hours for $19.1M won. The 4 A-class accounts returned $29.4M on 1,920 hours. → Bid price-shopper invitations only when the shop needs fill-in work; spend the hours on A-class GCs and on new accounts that look like them.
  4. Geography. Hit rate falls from 39% inside 60 miles to 22% beyond 250 (23 bids). Field hours on far jobs ran 25% over estimate. → Treat erection work beyond 250 miles as fab-only unless the GC is a relationship account; price per-diem crews explicitly when we do erect.
  5. Capacity and pipeline coverage. Won work still to fabricate is 2,286 tons, 16 weeks at 140 tons/week. Adding the probability-weighted pipeline, projected utilisation over the next four quarters is 2026Q3 77%, 2026Q4 66%, 2027Q1 49%, 2027Q2 46%, 2027Q3 35%. 4 of those quarters sit below 70 %. → The 29 Pursue opportunities carry 9,872 tons at 39% average win odds; to fill the open quarters the pipeline needs roughly 9,393 more tons of identified opportunities at today's hit rate.
  6. Estimated versus actual. Across 95 completed jobs we estimated 22.9% gross margin and realised 18.3%: 4.5 pts, about $4.3M of margin. Shop hours ran 10% over estimate and field hours 8%; complexity 5 work slipped most (10.6 pts). → Feed realised margin by project type and complexity back into the estimate as a line-item contingency instead of a flat markup.
  7. How much to trust the win odds. Out-of-time test on 100 bids after 2025-07-01: AUC 0.75, Brier 0.182 against 0.206 for always guessing the base rate. The model separates good bets from bad ones; it does not call individual bids. Use it to rank and to budget estimating hours, not to skip a job the room believes in. → Re-fit quarterly; the model gets better with every decided bid and every low-bid number we collect.
